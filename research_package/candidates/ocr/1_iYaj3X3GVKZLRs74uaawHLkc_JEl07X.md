Measurement of the refractive index and thermal refractive
index coefficients of Nd:YAP crystal

Zhnengdong Zeng, Hongyuan Shen, Miaoliang Huang, Hao Xu, Ruirong Zeng, Yuping Zhou, Guifang Yu,

and Chenghui Huang

The principal refractive indices of yttrium aluminate crystal doping with 1-wt. % neodymium (Nd:YAP) have
been measured by a minimum deviation method for the wavelengths 0.5398, 0.6328, and 1.0795 ym in the
temperature range of 289-435 K. The thermal refractive index coefficients have been obtained at these
wavelengths, and they are all positive and slightly decrease with increased wavelength. The constants of the
single term Sellmeier equation are also given for the 273-420 K temperature range. These constants are in
linear relationship with temperature in the 4 X 10~° error range.

Introduction

The refractive index and thermal refractive index
coefficient are important parameters in the design of a
_ solid state laser. The values of the refractive indices
_ for Nd:YAP crystal have been reported,!° but the

results presented are different. The thermal refrac-
_ tive index coefficients for n, and n, were only reported
_ by Young et al.4 at 0.6328 um. For other wavelengths,
_ no similar results have been reported so far. In this
_ paper, the improved autocollimation method is used to
_ measure the refractive indices of Nd:YAP crystal with
an accuracy of +1.5 X 10-4. To obtain refractive indi-
ces at the 287-485 K temperature range for wave-
lengths of 0.5398, 0.6328, and 1.0795 wm, the sample
temperature is controlled with an accuracy better than
41°C. It is shown that the thermal refractive index
_ coefficients of Nd: YAP crystal are all positive and that
_ they slightly decrease as the wavelength increases.
